Privacy Policy

Cardio Dash · Last updated: July 28, 2026
The short version: Cardio Dash does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Everything the app does happens entirely on your device. Your camera is used only to detect your movement in real time, and those camera images never leave your phone.

1. Who We Are

Cardio Dash ("the app," "we," "us") is a camera-controlled fitness game. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

2. Camera Use

Cardio Dash uses your device's front-facing camera to track your body movements (such as stepping, jumping, crouching, and jogging) so you can control the game with your body. This is the core function of the app.

  • Camera frames are processed in real time, on your device only.
  • Camera images are never recorded, saved, uploaded, or transmitted anywhere. They exist only momentarily in your device's memory to detect motion, and are immediately discarded.
  • We do not have access to your camera feed, and neither does any third party.

3. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically, Cardio Dash does not collect or process:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or contact details
  • Your location
  • Photos, videos, or camera recordings
  • Account information (the app has no accounts or logins)
  • Usage analytics or advertising identifiers

4. Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves a small amount of information locally on your device only, such as your high score, number of runs, and game settings. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not accessible to us or anyone else
  • Can be erased at any time by deleting the app

5. Third Parties

Cardio Dash does not share data with third parties, because it does not collect any. The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software.

6. Children's Privacy

Cardio Dash does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app is safe for all ages with respect to data, as no data is gathered or transmitted.

7. Internet Access

The app may download a small motion-detection model the first time it runs so it can recognize your movements. This download contains no personal information and is used solely to enable on-device motion tracking. Once loaded, the game runs without needing to send any of your information over the internet.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a revised "Last updated" date.
