Privacy Policy

Cardio Dash · Last updated: October 30, 2026
The short version: Cardio Dash has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, or location. Your camera is used only to detect your movement in real time, and those camera images never leave your phone. If you play Full Cardio mode, your best score is sent to our leaderboard under a randomly generated nickname (or a name you choose, with Cardio Dash Pro). If you subscribe to Pro, the purchase is handled by Apple and our subscription provider, RevenueCat.

1. Who We Are

Cardio Dash ("the app," "we," "us") is a camera-controlled fitness game. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

2. Camera Use

Cardio Dash uses your device's front-facing camera to track your body movements (such as stepping, jumping, crouching, and jogging) so you can control the game with your body. This is the core function of the app.

  • Camera frames are processed in real time, on your device only.
  • Camera images are never recorded, saved, uploaded, or transmitted anywhere. They exist only momentarily in your device's memory to detect motion, and are immediately discarded.
  • We do not have access to your camera feed, and neither does any third party.

3. Information We Collect

Cardio Dash has no accounts or logins. The only information that leaves your device is for the global leaderboard, which is used when you finish a Full Cardio run:

  • Gameplay content: your score (distance) for that run
  • A user ID: a random identifier generated by the app the first time it needs it, and an automatically generated nickname (for example, "Swift Falcon 4821"). Neither is based on your name, email, phone, Apple ID, or any other personal detail.
  • Optional (Cardio Dash Pro): a leaderboard name you type yourself, and the country you choose for country rankings. Both are shown to other players on the leaderboards. Do not use your real name or any personal information as a leaderboard name. Your country is chosen once and is not taken from your location.
  • Per-map scores: your best distance on each map, used for map rankings.
  • Purchase information (Cardio Dash Pro): if you subscribe, Apple processes your payment and we never see your payment details. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receives your random user ID and your purchase and subscription status so the app can unlock Pro features and let you restore purchases.

This information is used only to show and rank leaderboard scores (app functionality). It is not used for advertising, analytics, or tracking, and it is not sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers. Like any internet service, our hosting provider may temporarily process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the request. Quick Play mode does not submit scores.

Cardio Dash does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or contact details
  • Your precise location
  • Photos, videos, or camera recordings
  • Advertising identifiers or usage analytics

To request removal of your leaderboard entry, or to remove a name or country, email us at the address below with your nickname. You can also stop being ranked by not playing Full Cardio mode.

4. Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves information locally on your device, such as your high score, number of runs, streak, game settings, and the random leaderboard ID and nickname above. If you allow notifications, daily reminders are scheduled on your device only. Local data can be erased at any time by deleting the app.

5. Third Parties

Leaderboard data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, which processes it on our behalf to operate the leaderboard. Subscription purchases are processed by Apple, and purchase status is managed by RevenueCat on our behalf. The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software, and we do not share leaderboard data with anyone else.

6. Children's Privacy

Cardio Dash does not ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children. The free leaderboard uses only a random ID, an automatically generated nickname, and a score. Pro players can choose their own leaderboard name; names are filtered, other players can report or hide a name, and reported names are reviewed and removed. Children should not enter personal information in a leaderboard name.

7. Internet Access

The motion-detection model is bundled with the app and runs entirely on your device, so the game itself works without an internet connection. An internet connection is used only to submit and view leaderboard scores and to manage purchases; if you are offline, the game still works and the leaderboard simply isn't shown.

If this Privacy Policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a revised "Last updated" date.
